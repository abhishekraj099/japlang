# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

Manabi ("the app", "we") is a Japanese learning app. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. We wrote it to match exactly what the app does today.

## The short version

- Everything you do in the app is stored only on your phone. We do not run a server, and we do not receive your data.
- We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.
- A few features use services from Google (speech recognition, handwriting recognition, YouTube videos). When you use them, those services handle data under Google's own privacy policy.
- You can delete everything at any time from Settings → Delete all my data.

## Information stored on your phone

The app saves the following on your device only:

- **Account details you enter:** your name and email address. Sign-in currently happens only on your phone: your password is not checked, stored or sent anywhere.
- **Learning progress:** lessons finished, XP, streak, your tree, review schedules for words and characters, test scores, and video practice scores.
- **Settings:** daily goal, theme, guide character, sound and voice preferences, reminder time.
- **Usage log:** a record of events inside the app (for example "lesson finished" or "review done") that helps us improve lessons. It stays on your phone and is never uploaded.
- **Crash log:** if the app crashes, technical details of the error are saved on your phone. It is only shared if you choose to send it to us.
- **Problem reports** you write about a lesson are saved on your phone.

If your phone's Android backup is turned on, Android may include this app data in your device backup to your Google account. This is controlled by your phone settings, not by us.

## Microphone and speaking practice

Speaking exercises use your microphone to check your pronunciation. Your speech is sent to the speech recognition service installed on your phone (usually Google), which turns it into text. That service may process the audio on its servers under its own privacy policy. The app itself does not record, keep or upload your audio. You can refuse the microphone permission; speaking exercises will then be skipped or use the system speech dialog.

## Handwriting

Writing exercises use Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition. The recognition runs on your phone. The first time you write, the app downloads Google's Japanese handwriting model. Google ML Kit may collect limited technical and usage information about the feature, as described in Google's ML Kit terms. Your drawings are not uploaded by the app.

## Voices

Lesson audio and your guide's voice use the text-to-speech engine on your phone, or recordings bundled with the app.

## Videos

The Video tab plays clips from YouTube inside the app using YouTube's embedded player, and loads video thumbnails from YouTube. When you watch, YouTube (Google) receives information such as your IP address and may use cookies, under the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy. Videos and their subtitles belong to their creators.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your phone. No data is sent anywhere.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings → Delete all my data** erases your account details, progress, settings, usage log and crash log from the app.
- **Settings → Reset progress** erases only your learning progress.
- Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your phone.

## Security

Your data stays inside the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read.

## Changes to this policy

If the app starts sending data anywhere, for example when online accounts are added, we will update this policy before that change, and show the new effective date.
